FAQ · Updated: 4 August 2026

Short answers to common questions about Dashday. If this doesn’t help, contact us below.

Where are my todos stored?

On your device by default (SwiftData). Optional cloud sync also stores them in our Supabase project, protected by row-level security so only your account can access them.

Do I need an account?

No. Dashday works offline-first without signing in. An account is only needed if you want sync across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Watch.

How do I delete my sync account?

In the app under Settings → Delete Account. That permanently removes server-side todos and the auth account. When signing out you can keep or clear local todos.

Apple Watch, Siri, widgets & Action Button

The Watch app supports dictation and save even without the iPhone nearby. Siri/Shortcuts (“Dashday”, “for tomorrow…”), Home/Lock Screen widgets, and the Action Button (microphone) are covered in the app and iOS settings. Spotlight indexes open todos locally.

What is the AI morning briefing?

An optional on-device summary of open todos at a time you choose — using Apple Intelligence / Foundation Models, with no server. Notifications and a supported device are required.

Backup to iCloud or Google Drive

Optional daily CSV export to an iCloud folder you choose, or via Google OAuth (drive.file) into a Dashday folder in your Drive. You manage those files yourself.

Repeating tasks

Completing a repeating todo creates the next occurrence. Set frequency and series in the todo details.

Forgot password / email not confirmed

In Settings → Sign in you’ll find “Forgot password?” and, if needed, “Resend confirmation”. The email link opens Dashday (or the website as a fallback).
